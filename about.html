about.exe // on and off the clock
hi, i'm elijah

Systems person. Restless traveler. Recovering English major.

I run revenue operations and analytics at Walker Sands, a B2B agency in Chicago. In plain terms: when a leadership team asks "what's actually driving pipeline?", I make sure there's a straight answer. I do that for dozens of clients, across every CRM you can name.

I came at this sideways. English degree, a stint as an assistant editor, then a master's in curriculum and instructional leadership. Editing taught me to spot where a structure breaks. Teaching taught me that a system nobody can learn is a system nobody uses.

The rest of the time I'm usually somewhere new. The job travels well, so some weeks the office is a hammock, a vineyard, or whichever café has the best Wi-Fi.
